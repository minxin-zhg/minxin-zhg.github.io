About

I am a Research Scientist at Z.ai in Beijing, where I work on optimization for large-scale AI and language model training.

Previously, I was a Hedrick Assistant Adjunct Professor in the Department of Mathematics at UCLA, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Hayden Schaeffer and Prof. Deanna Needell.

I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics at the University of California, San Diego, under the supervision of Prof. Philip E. Gill. I previously received a Master of Mathematics from the University of Waterloo in Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Kirsten Morris, and a B.S. in Mathematics from Wuhan University in China. I also interned at Alibaba's Decision Intelligence Lab (Sunnyvale, CA) in summer 2022.

Research

Research interests

My work combines optimization theory, scalable algorithm design, and computation. Currently, I am especially interested in optimization algorithms for AI and large language model training.

01

Optimization for AI

Adaptive and matrix-aware optimization methods for efficient, stable large-scale model training.

02

Mathematical optimization

Constrained, nonconvex, stochastic, and global optimization algorithms with rigorous convergence analysis.

03

Scientific computing

Randomized numerical linear algebra, tensor computation, and optimization for scientific problems.
